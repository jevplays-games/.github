# Code of conduct

We want these projects to be a respectful place to build and play.

**Expected:** be courteous, assume good faith, give specific and kind feedback, and accept corrections about your own work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexual or hateful content, or publishing others' private information.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any community space tied to these repositories. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation to enforce it.

Report concerns to Timothy.Gregg@complete.tech (Complete Tech LLC). Reports are handled privately.
